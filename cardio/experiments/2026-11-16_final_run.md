# Final run of the heart results

Mon 16 Nov, Aviral, roadmap S5. Command, from the repository root on the laptop
that holds `Real_World_Data`:

```bash
python -m cardio.run_all --final --clinic-dir <path to Real_World_Data>
```

The branch held every heart script: Shivansh's official S2 to S4 work (PRs #14
and #18), Anshuman's calibration and late fusion (Tasks 4 and 5, from `main`),
and `cardio/predict.py`. `--final` found no missing code.

## What ran

Twelve steps ran in order in 21 minutes and none failed: cross-validation
intervals, characteristics (Yaseen and PhysioNet), votes, calibration, late
fusion, robustness, agreement, re-quantisation (PhysioNet and clinic), the edge
budget, and the clinic recordings with adaptation. The three BMD-HS steps
(characteristics, unseen datasets, adaptation) need data that is on Shivansh's
laptop; his official outputs from 15 Nov stay as committed.

## What reproduced

- **Identical:** `heart_cv_yaseen.json`, `heart_agreement.json`,
  `heart_calibration.json`, `heart_fusion.json`, `heart_robustness.json` and
  `cardio/data/circor_folds.csv`.
- **Rounding only:** the four CSVs of characteristics and votes differ by at most
  5 × 10⁻¹⁴ and no missing value moved, so the committed files were kept.
- **Clinic recordings:** every total, average and interval in
  `heart_real_world.json`, and every agreement in `heart_requantisation.json`,
  reproduced. Only the run date and the times changed. So the switch of
  `real_world.py` to the team's shared `cardio/tta/bn_adapt.py` (PR #13) left the
  clinic results unchanged. The run is logged in the `Real_World_Data` README.
- **Edge budget:** median time per case 16.7 ms on Yaseen fold 0 and 97 ms on
  the PhysioNet sample (95th percentile 202 ms), against 10.5 and 80 ms on
  15 Nov on the same laptop: wall-clock times vary with the laptop's load. Every
  target is still met, by a wide margin. T = 0.504 is applied.
